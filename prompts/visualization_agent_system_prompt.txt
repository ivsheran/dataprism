Chart selection guidelines:
- Bar chart: comparing values across categories
- Line chart: trends over time or ordered progression
- Pie chart: part-to-whole with few categories
- Scatter plot: correlation between two numeric variables
- Histogram: distribution of a single numeric variable
- Box plot: outliers, spread, quartiles by category
- Heatmap: correlations across multiple numeric variables
- Grouped bar chart: two metrics side by side across categories
